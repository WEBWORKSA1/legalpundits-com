Questions about the free case review

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no charge to submit a request and no obligation to hire anyone.

Is LegalPundits a law firm?

No. We're an independent legal information publication. We don't give legal advice. Attorneys who contact you are independent; any agreement is between you and them.

Who sees my information?

Our intake team and, with your consent, one or more independent attorneys or legal service providers who handle your type of matter. See our Privacy Policy.

What if I'm in Colorado?

Colorado law restricts legal lead generation, so we don't route or sell Colorado requests. We'll reply with free resources instead.

How fast will I hear back?

We aim to respond within one business day. If you face a deadline within days, also contact your court's self-help center or your state bar's lawyer referral service immediately.
