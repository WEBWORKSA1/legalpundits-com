Global Law Desk

LegalPundits focuses on US law, but legal questions are global. Start with these official and non-profit resources in your country.

🇺🇸 United States

Laws differ by state. Start with your state court's self-help center and your state bar's lawyer referral service.

  • LawHelp.org — free legal aid by state
  • USA.gov — find legal aid
  • ABA Free Legal Answers
  • Legal Services Corporation
  • Cornell LII — free US law

🇨🇦 Canada

Family, property and employment law are mostly provincial; criminal law is federal.

  • CanLII — free Canadian case law & legislation
  • Department of Justice Canada
  • Steps to Justice (Ontario)
  • Éducaloi (Québec)

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

England & Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have separate legal systems.

  • Citizens Advice
  • GOV.UK — check if you can get legal aid
  • Law Society — find a solicitor
  • legislation.gov.uk

🇮🇳 India

Free legal aid is available to eligible people through Legal Services Authorities at national, state and district level.

  • NALSA — free legal services
  • India Code — central acts
  • eCourts Services
  • Supreme Court of India

🇦🇺 Australia

Each state and territory has its own legal aid commission and court system.

  • National Legal Aid
  • AustLII — free Australian law
  • Federal Register of Legislation

Links go to official government, court, bar or non-profit sources. LegalPundits is not affiliated with these organisations.
