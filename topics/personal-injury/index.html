Quick answers

Should I call the police after a minor accident?

In many states you must report crashes involving injury, death or property damage above a set dollar amount. Even when not required, a police report gives you an independent record that helps with insurance.

What is the average personal injury settlement?

There is no meaningful single average because values depend on injuries, fault, venue and insurance limits. Minor soft-tissue claims often resolve for a few thousand dollars, while surgery cases can reach six or seven figures.

Can I sue if I fell in a store?

Possibly, if the store created the hazard or failed to find and fix it within a reasonable time. Simply falling is not enough; you need evidence of the hazard and the store's notice.

Can a statute of limitations be extended?

Not by agreement after the fact in most cases, but tolling rules (minority, incapacity, discovery, absence) can delay or pause it. A lawyer can tell you whether any exception applies.
