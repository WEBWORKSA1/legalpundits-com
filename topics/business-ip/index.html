Quick answers

Which state should I form my LLC in?

Usually your home state, where you'll operate anyway. Delaware is popular for venture-backed corporations, but forming elsewhere often means registering in your home state too.

Do I need to register a trademark?

No, use creates some rights, but federal registration gives much stronger, nationwide protection.
