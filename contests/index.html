Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Residents of Quebec are not eligible.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals 18+ (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). Employees of LegalPundits and their immediate families are not eligible.
  3. Entry period. Each contest runs from the 1st to the last day of the calendar month (11:59 p.m. US Eastern). One entry per person per contest per month.
  4. Judging. These are skill-based contests. Entries are judged by the LegalPundits editorial team on accuracy (40%), clarity (30%), usefulness (20%) and originality (10%). Quiz leaderboard prizes go to the highest verified score; ties are broken by earliest submission.
  5. Prizes. Prizes are as listed for the month. Winners are notified by email within 15 days after the month ends and must respond within 14 days. Prizes may be substituted with equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes.
  6. Content. Entries must be original, accurate and must not contain confidential client information or legal advice to a specific person. By entering, you grant LegalPundits a non-exclusive licence to publish and promote your entry with credit.
  7. Privacy. Entry information is used as described in our Privacy Policy.
