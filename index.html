About LegalPundits

LegalPundits.com is an independent legal information publication. We translate statutes, court rules and agency guidance into plain English, build free tools that answer the questions people actually ask ("How long do I have?", "What's my claim worth?", "What will I take home?"), and point you to the right kind of help, whether that's a free legal aid office or an experienced attorney. We're not a law firm, and nothing here is legal advice, but it's the fastest way to understand where you stand.

Outside the US? Start with our Global Law Desk for Canada, the UK, India and Australia.
