Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information LegalPundits.com collects, how it's used and the choices you have. Last updated: November 2026.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (case review, question, newsletter, donation pledge, contest entry, job application, attorney or advertising enquiry), we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone, location and description. Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit).

How we use it

  • To respond to you and provide what you asked for.
  • For case reviews you authorise: to share your submission with independent attorneys or legal service providers who may contact you about your matter.
  • To send newsletters you've opted into (you can unsubscribe any time).
  • To run contests, process applications and improve the site.

We do not sell newsletter lists. We do not sell or route Colorado legal leads.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our partners use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences (such as dark mode), measure usage and serve ads.

Advertising and Google AdSense

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Embedded content

Pages may embed YouTube videos. YouTube may set cookies and collect data under Google's privacy policy when you play an embedded video.

Your choices and rights

You can block or delete cookies in your browser, decline the optional cookie notice, and ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you submitted. Residents of certain regions (such as the EEA, UK, California and other US states) may have additional rights under local law. Contact us through the contact form to make a request.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards, but no transmission over the internet is completely secure. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
