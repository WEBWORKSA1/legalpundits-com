## Key findings that shape the build

1. **Money is in intent, not traffic.** Attorneys & Legal Services is the most expensive Google Ads category in 2026 (avg CPC $9.87 vs $5.42 all-industry; CPL $131.63 vs $66.69). Content that ranks for accident, injury, employment, divorce, DUI, immigration and debt queries earns the highest AdSense RPMs.
2. **Lead value dwarfs ad value.** Shared PI leads sell for roughly $50–$150, exclusive ones $200–$400+. One qualified case-review submission can out-earn thousands of ad impressions — so the funnel is the core product and AdSense is the base layer.
3. **Incumbents are text-heavy.** Calculator-first, modern-UI sites (divorce.law pattern) win engagement. LegalPundits leads with free tools.
4. **Regulatory trap:** Colorado SB26-174 (effective 12 Aug 2026) prohibits paying for, engaging in, or selling legal lead-generation leads in Colorado. The funnel flags Colorado submissions as "do not sell" and routes them to free resources.
5. **Trust signals are mandatory** for YMYL legal content and AdSense approval: named review dates, official-source citations, "not a law firm" disclaimers, editorial policy, transparent ads/affiliate disclosure.
6. **Name conflict check:** "Law Pundits Global" (legal-education courses) uses a similar name. LegalPundits.com disclaims affiliation and uses the name only as its domain identity.
