About LegalPundits

LegalPundits.com exists for one reason: most people facing a legal problem can't get a straight answer fast. We fix that with plain-English explanations, free interactive tools and a simple way to reach real legal help.

What we do

  • Explain. In-depth guides on the legal problems people actually search for: accidents, jobs, divorce, arrests, immigration, wills, housing, debt and business.
  • Equip. Free calculators and generators: settlement estimates, filing deadlines, fee math, court-day counts and demand letters.
  • Connect. A free case review that can put you in touch with independent attorneys, and directions to free legal aid when that's the better fit.
  • Keep you current. A legal news desk and video library that refresh automatically several times a day.

Who we are

LegalPundits is an independent publication. We are not a law firm, and our writers and editors do not provide legal advice or create attorney-client relationships. Our content is researched from primary sources (statutes, court rules and official government guidance) and reviewed under our editorial policy.

How we're funded

We're free to read. We're supported by advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, attorney-network partnerships and reader support. Advertisers never influence our explanations. See our disclaimer and advertising disclosure.
