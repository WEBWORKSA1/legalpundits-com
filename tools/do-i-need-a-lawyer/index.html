Do I Need a Lawyer? Quiz

Six quick questions to gauge how urgently you should speak with an attorney.

1. What kind of problem is it?
2. Is there a court date, legal deadline or government notice?
3. Roughly how much is at stake (money, property or benefits)?
4. Does the other side have a lawyer or insurance adjuster?
5. Could you lose your freedom, children, home, job or immigration status?
6. Have you been asked to sign anything (settlement, release, plea, severance)?

How it works

Each answer adds points for urgency and stakes. Criminal exposure, near deadlines, high stakes, a represented opponent and documents awaiting signature all raise the score.

FAQs

Is this quiz legal advice?

No. It's a general triage tool to help you decide your next step.

What if I can't afford a lawyer?

Check free legal aid, bar referral services with low-cost consultations, and contingency-fee lawyers for injury and some employment cases.

What's the fastest way to talk to someone?

Submit a free case review, and if you have a court date, call your court's self-help center too.
