How it works

Contingency fees are a percentage of the recovery. Costs (filing fees, records, experts) and liens (health insurers, hospitals, Medicare) are usually repaid from the settlement too. Your written fee agreement controls the actual math.

FAQs

Are contingency percentages negotiable?

Often. Some lawyers offer sliding scales, lower percentages for quick settlements or caps for large recoveries.

Can medical liens be reduced?

Frequently. Lawyers often negotiate liens down, which can significantly increase what you keep.

What if we lose?

Under a typical contingency agreement you owe no fee. Check whether you'd still owe case costs.
