FAQs

What if my deadline has passed?

Talk to a lawyer anyway. Tolling rules (for minors, late discovery or a defendant's absence) sometimes extend deadlines.

Does negotiating with the insurer stop the clock?

No. Only filing a lawsuit (or a valid tolling agreement) protects you against the statute of limitations.

Why are government claims different?

Most states require a written notice of claim within a short period, sometimes 30 to 180 days, before you can sue a public entity.
