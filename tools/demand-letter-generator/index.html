FAQs

Is a demand letter legally required?

Not always, but some claims and many small claims courts expect you to request payment first, and a clear letter often resolves the dispute.

How should I send it?

Use a trackable method such as certified mail with return receipt, plus email if you normally communicate that way.

What if they ignore it?

Consider small claims court. See our small claims guide.
